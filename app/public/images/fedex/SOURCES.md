### Location and service information

The five saved New York examples in `src/lib/locations.ts` use addresses, public business phone numbers, and listing URLs from the [official New York directory](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ny/new-york), reviewed October 13, 2026. They are an explicitly curated snapshot, not a live, complete, geolocated, or nearest-location feed. No invented distance, opening status, or map is displayed. Official listings and Google Maps links provide the real handoff.

Eligibility for complimentary packaging is based on [FedEx packing supplies](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies.html). Pickup handoff URLs come from [FedEx pickup options](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/schedule-manage-pickups.html). Support, claims, phone-number, and FAQ handoffs come from [official customer support](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support.html). The local estimator is an illustrative formula, not FedEx pricing or a delivery commitment. The print planner checks filename/type/size only, downloads a local text brief, and does not send file contents or place an order.

The iOS and Android links on the tracking page are the App Store and Google Play destinations published on the [official Union Square location page](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ny/new-york/office-4483).

Roboto is self-hosted separately in `public/fonts/` under the included license. Older stand-in assets outside this directory remain for untouched tools; the refreshed homepage and interior pages above only use the genuine images listed here.
